# Home page override

The page follows the supplied Superlative industrial-product monograph reference over the database's generic Bento recommendation.

- Compact full-bleed video hero with a practical service headline, primary estimate CTA, and visible links to all five main site sections.
- Warm paper `#f3f0ea`, ink `#11110f`, vapor `#f8f7f3`, single ember accent `#e66f27`.
- Display hierarchy comes from Manrope's geometric scale and light weight; labels use condensed Roboto Condensed. Supporting-page headings stay moderate and service-oriented rather than poster-sized.
- Controls use 1px outlines and capsule geometry; process indexes and image portals add controlled circular forms. No filled accent CTA, decorative gradients, or shadows.
- Product/process imagery carries the color; surrounding UI stays achromatic.
- Motion adds restrained one-shot hero staging, one-shot section reveals, and subtle image parallax; no preloader, pinning, or text parallax.
- Hero footage is synchronized to the project wall clock, not page lifetime, so reloads preserve its global phase.
- Continuous video pauses by default for users who prefer reduced motion.
- Hero staging and GSAP sequences are skipped for users who prefer reduced motion.
- Service content is arranged as compact grids, specification rows, and hairline-separated lists. Avoid empty editorial plates and rounded marketing cards.
- The commercial sequence is fixed: services first, materials second, then typical tasks, process, estimate form, and FAQ.
- Every primary service and material has a dedicated landing page; summaries on the home and catalog pages link directly to those routes.
- Do not add manifesto sections or full-viewport text plates. Each section must help a visitor choose a service, material, or next action.
- FAQ uses native `details`/`summary`; technical tables collapse to readable single-column rows on small screens.
- The supplied production footage is reserved for the home hero. Supporting pages and case cards use distinct licensed production photography matched to their subject.
