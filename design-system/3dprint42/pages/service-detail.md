# Service detail page override

Service pages are information-first commercial landings, not promotional posters.

- Keep the hero compact: service name, useful lead, short explanation, and two actions only.
- Follow with a persistent horizontal contents index for fast access to the page sections.
- Required sequence: overview and use cases, scope of work, workflow, order parameters, source-data checklist and limitations, relevant materials, FAQ, request form.
- Use 15–18px body copy, moderate 30–45px section headings, compact hairline-separated grids, and multiple useful facts within each desktop viewport.
- Avoid single-line full-width cards, oversized headings, empty sections, and decorative copy that does not help estimate or choose the service.
- Every service-specific claim lives in `app/site-content.ts`; do not hardcode different business rules in the route component.
- Do not invent fixed lead times, tolerances, prices, or material performance values without confirmed production data.
